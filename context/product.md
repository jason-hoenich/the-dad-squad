# The Dad Squad Product Context

## What It Is

The Dad Squad is a rent-a-dad handyman service. The landing page markets home repair, furniture builds, and general fixes to homeowners in the Silver Lake, Atwater Village, Los Feliz, and Glendale neighborhoods of Los Angeles.

## Brand

- Warm, approachable, dad-energy tone
- Color palette: warm cream, dad orange (#E8722A), dark brown, sage green
- Typography: Fraunces (headings) + DM Sans (body)
- Tagline style: neighborhood handyman vibe, not corporate contractor

## Current State

Single landing page deployed on Vercel. No backend, no booking system, no payments. Contact is likely via phone/text or a contact form.

## Service Areas

Silver Lake, Atwater Village, Los Feliz, Glendale (Los Angeles, CA)

## Tech

- One index.html file with everything inline
- Static images in repo root
- Vercel hosting with vercel.json config
- No dependencies, no build process
